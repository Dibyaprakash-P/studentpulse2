Features

Everything You Need to Succeed

Powerful tools designed specifically for student life — from burnout prevention to homework management.

AI Burnout Prediction

Machine learning models analyze your patterns and predict burnout risk before it happens. Get real-time risk scores and prevention tips.

Homework Tracker

Add homework with deadlines, subjects, and priority levels. Mark complete, filter by status, and never miss a submission again.

Smart Notes

Capture ideas, lecture notes, and reminders. Color-coded, pinnable, and searchable — everything organized in one place.

Project Manager

Add projects with deadlines, attach files in any format, add tags, and track progress from planning to completion.

Parent Monitoring

Parents link accounts to monitor their child's wellness, stress levels, burnout risk, and academic health in real-time.

Gamification & XP

Earn badges, maintain streaks, and level up as you build healthy habits. Compete with yourself and stay motivated.

Productivity Analytics

Weekly charts showing study time, sleep quality, stress vs energy trends, and productivity grades to track improvement.

Cross-Platform

Available on Android, iOS, macOS, and Windows. Your data syncs everywhere — use it at home, at school, or on the go.
